I’m at the beginning of my journey in app development and software engineering. There’s a lot I’m still learning, and that’s part of what makes building exciting.

I created a Muslim app called Rahmah. It’s a step toward the kind of work I want to do: making ideas real and creating something that matters to people.

My ambition is to become a self-made entrepreneur and, one day, a CEO. For now, I’m focused on the work that comes first — learning, practicing, and building.
